# Pre-registration of the evaluation rules (version 1, DRAFT)

Date: 2026-10-22 (the date counts from the commit that adds this file to `main` and from its OpenTimestamps proof). Status: **draft for approval by the owner; it is only binding once merged to `main`.** The live series started on 2026-10-19; official snapshots (00, 06, 12, 18 UTC) are logged in `ledger/`.

These rules are fixed before any "the AI works" statement is made. A change is a new version of this document (new file, new date); results are then reported per version and the day count starts again (R11).

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| R1 | The time axis is the snapshot time, not the model run number. A public model contributes to a snapshot only a run that was available (`available_at`) at the snapshot time; until the availability times are measured, conservative delays apply (ICON-D2 about 2 h, ECMWF about 7 h, ALADIN 3–4.5 h). The same rule holds for the AI and the blend. |
| R2 | Lead bands by `valid_time − snapshot time` (D+1 = 24–47 h and so on). D+1 to D+3 are the main scores; D+4 and D+5 are secondary. |
| R3 | Only common pairs count (district or station, hour, variable) for every compared series: no filling, no substitution by an older run. The number of excluded pairs is reported. |
| R4 | A fixed, versioned list of stations and district pools (hash at the date of this document); a change is a new version. |
| R5 | Observations: ČHMÚ only, the same cleaning for all series. Scores are marked *provisional* until the data are quality-controlled, then *final*. |
| R6 | Two hypotheses stated in advance: (1) the AI beats our blend, (2) the AI beats the best public model. Measured by MAE of Tmax, Tmin, hourly temperature and wind, lead D+1. More tests are corrected (Holm). |
| R7 | Labels: fewer than 30 days of live snapshots *not enough data*; 30–59 days *preliminary*; at least 60 days, a 95 % confidence interval against the blend entirely below zero and stability across months are needed before saying "works" (about 2026-12-18 at the earliest). |
| R8 | Block bootstrap over 4-day blocks, sensitivity with 14-day blocks. |
| R9 | Live AI and hindcast are kept apart; a hindcast is never counted as verification. |
| R10 | A missing snapshot (failure) is counted and published in the outage log; it is never filled in afterwards. |
| R11 | A model change means a new `model_version`; scores per version, the day counter restarts at zero; no choosing of the best period. |
| R12 | Weak spots are shown: the worst 5 days of the last 30 next to the best ones; 2026-10-19/20 Tmax (the AI ranked 11th and 12th of 21) stays as a documented example. |

Metrics: MAE (main), bias, RMSE, rank of N; precipitation: Brier score and Brier skill against climatology, POD/FAR/ETS (at least 20 events); operations: share of snapshots in time.
