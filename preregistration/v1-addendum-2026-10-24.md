# Addendum to the pre-registration v1 (2026-10-24)

This is not a change of the rules R1–R12 in `v1.md` (that file is left as it was committed).

- From the snapshot of 24 Oct 2026 00:00 UTC the ledger uses policy `public-v2`: the weights of the blend are not published, only their sha256 is in the ledger row (`files`, `withheld`).
- The first four snapshots (23 Oct 2026, policy `public-v1`) had published the weights. Their releases were taken down; the ledger rows stay in the chain unchanged (`ledger/withdrawn.json`). They can no longer be downloaded, re-checked or scored, so scoring starts with the first `public-v2` snapshot. Nothing was claimed from those four snapshots.
- Earlier test releases (`test-*`) were deleted; they were never part of the ledger chain.
